==============================================================================
 LIVE DRIVE 4 - the setting is a home setting: it converges to second mates
==============================================================================

A primary firstmate home with one registered local second mate. The second
mate owns two children of its own: phase8 has a recorded start, phase9 has none.

--- the primary turns the setting on, then converges it --------------------
$ ls mate/config/            # before
  (empty)

$ bin/fm-config-push.sh

  secondmate domain-alpha (/private/var/folders/ym/vnmfjh5n7dn2vdzdgy7zrbjh0000gn/T/fm-live-drive.SJJJnu/secondmate/mate):
    crew-dispatch.json: unchanged
    crew-harness: unchanged
    backlog-backend: unchanged
    backend: unchanged

  CONFIG_REREAD: secondmate domain-alpha: send failed: error: no-mistakes gate agent must not drive the fleet (NO_MISTAKES_GATE set)

$ ls mate/config/            # after
  worker-running-time

--- the child's running time is measured by the home that OWNS the child ----
$ jq '.active_children[]' mate/state/home-summary.json     # the mate's own ledger
  {"id":"phase8","running_seconds":4872}
  {"id":"phase9","running_seconds":null}

$ bin/fm-bearings-snapshot.sh   (run in the PRIMARY home)
  in_flight[3]{id,kind,state,repo,running,doing}:
    local-ship,ship,working,firstmate,7m,harness busy (claude-hook)
    domain-alpha/phase8,ship,working,sample,1h 21m,harness busy (claude-hook)
    domain-alpha/phase9,ship,working,sample,unknown,harness busy (claude-hook)
  -> the child row carries the elapsed time its own home recorded, and the
     child with no recorded start reads 'unknown' rather than a guess.

--- adversarial: the primary is on but the mate has not converged yet -------
$ jq '.active_children[]|has("running_seconds")' mate/state/home-summary.json
  [false,false]
$ bin/fm-bearings-snapshot.sh   (PRIMARY - local row has a value, child rows do not)
  in_flight[3]{id,kind,state,repo,running,doing}:
    local-ship,ship,working,firstmate,7m,harness busy (claude-hook)
    domain-alpha/phase8,ship,working,sample,unknown,harness busy (claude-hook)
    domain-alpha/phase9,ship,working,sample,unknown,harness busy (claude-hook)
  -> the local row and both child rows still share ONE column set. The TOON
     encoder takes its columns from the first row, so a producer disagreeing
     with the projection would silently misalign every row after it.

--- adversarial: the mate is on but the primary is not ----------------------
$ bin/fm-bearings-snapshot.sh   (PRIMARY)
  in_flight[3]{id,kind,state,repo,doing}:
    local-ship,ship,working,firstmate,harness busy (claude-hook)
    domain-alpha/phase8,ship,working,sample,harness busy (claude-hook)
    domain-alpha/phase9,ship,working,sample,harness busy (claude-hook)
  -> no running column anywhere: the home reading the report decides.
