### lab-ship brief: Firstmate instruction inbox section
# Firstmate instruction inbox
Firstmate steers you through durable message files in '$FM_HOME/state/lab-ship.inbox'.
When a terminal message says an instruction is waiting there - and at any natural checkpoint when you are unsure - list '$FM_HOME/state/lab-ship.inbox'/*.msg, read and act on each message in numeric order, then acknowledge each handled message by moving it: `mv '$FM_HOME/state/lab-ship.inbox'/NNN.msg '$FM_HOME/state/lab-ship.inbox'/handled/`.
The move IS the acknowledgement: without it firstmate rings again and eventually treats you as stuck. An empty or absent inbox needs no action.
Apart from your launch brief, every message firstmate puts in your terminal starts with `: Firstmate`.
Any other message typed into your terminal is the captain speaking to you directly: it is authoritative, so follow it, and first append his exact words, unedited, under a `## <UTC timestamp>` heading to '$FM_HOME/data/lab-ship/captain-direct.md' so firstmate can carry them into its own records.
